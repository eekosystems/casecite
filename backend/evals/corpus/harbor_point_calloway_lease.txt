COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (this "Lease") is made as of April 10, 2024 between Harbor Point Properties LLC, a Maine limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Calloway Bakery & Cafe, Inc., a Maine corporation ("Tenant").

SECTION 1. BASIC TERMS

1.1 Premises. Suite 110 of the building known as Harbor Point Commons, 2200 Wharf Street, Portland, Maine, containing approximately 2,400 rentable square feet (the "Premises"), as shown on Exhibit A.

1.2 Term. Five (5) years, commencing on June 1, 2024 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on May 31, 2029, unless sooner terminated or extended as provided in this Lease.

1.3 Permitted Use. Operation of a retail bakery and cafe, including the sale of baked goods, coffee, tea and light prepared foods for consumption on or off the Premises, and for no other purpose.

SECTION 2. RENT

2.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay annual base rent in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each calendar month, according to the following schedule:

| Lease Year | Annual Base Rent | Monthly Installment |
| 1 | $72,000.00 | $6,000.00 |
| 2 | $74,160.00 | $6,180.00 |
| 3 | $76,385.00 | $6,365.42 |
| 4 | $78,676.00 | $6,556.33 |
| 5 | $81,037.00 | $6,753.08 |

2.2 Late Charge. If any installment of rent is not received by Landlord within ten (10) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. The late charge is liquidated damages for Landlord's administrative costs and is not a penalty.

2.3 Additional Rent. All sums payable by Tenant under this Lease other than base rent are additional rent.

2.4 Security Deposit. On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of Eighteen Thousand Dollars ($18,000.00) as security for Tenant's performance. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance within thirty (30) days after the end of the term.

SECTION 3. OPERATING EXPENSES

3.1 Common Area Maintenance. Tenant shall pay Tenant's Proportionate Share of the costs of operating, maintaining and insuring the common areas of Harbor Point Commons. "Tenant's Proportionate Share" is eight and four-tenths percent (8.4%).

3.2 Estimates and Reconciliation. Landlord shall deliver an annual estimate of common area costs, and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate each month. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a reconciliation statement, and any overpayment shall be credited against the next installments of additional rent.

3.3 Exclusive Use. So long as Tenant is open and operating in the Premises and is not in default, Landlord shall not lease any other space in Harbor Point Commons to a tenant whose primary business is the sale of baked goods.

SECTION 4. MAINTENANCE AND ALTERATIONS

4.1 Tenant's Obligations. Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, including kitchen equipment, grease traps and storefront glass, in good order and repair.

4.2 Landlord's Obligations. Landlord shall maintain the roof, foundation, structural elements and the building systems serving the Premises, except for damage caused by Tenant.

4.3 Alterations. Tenant shall not make structural alterations without Landlord's prior written consent. Non-structural alterations costing less than Fifteen Thousand Dollars ($15,000.00) in the aggregate in any lease year require only prior written notice.

SECTION 5. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

5.1 Consent Required. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay.

5.2 Landlord's Discretion. Notwithstanding Section 5.1, Landlord may withhold its consent in its sole discretion to any proposed assignee whose tangible net worth is less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) as of the date of the proposed assignment.

5.3 No Release. No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease.

SECTION 6. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

6.1 Tenant's Insurance. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence, naming Landlord as an additional insured.

6.2 Indemnity. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against claims for injury or damage occurring in the Premises, except to the extent caused by the negligence of Landlord or its agents.

SECTION 7. CASUALTY

7.1 Restoration. If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Premises to substantially their condition at the Commencement Date, and rent shall abate in proportion to the area rendered untenantable.

7.2 Termination Right. If Landlord reasonably estimates that the Premises cannot be restored within one hundred eighty (180) days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice given within thirty (30) days after Landlord delivers its estimate.

SECTION 8. RENEWAL OPTION

8.1 Option. Provided Tenant is not then in default, Tenant has one (1) option to extend the term for an additional five (5) years (the "Renewal Term").

8.2 Exercise. Tenant must exercise the option by written notice to Landlord given not less than one hundred eighty (180) days and not more than three hundred sixty-five (365) days before the expiration of the initial term. A notice given outside that window is void.

8.3 Renewal Rent. Base rent for the first year of the Renewal Term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent for comparable retail space in Portland, Maine, and shall increase by three percent (3%) annually thereafter.

SECTION 9. DEFAULT

9.1 Events of Default. Each of the following is an event of default: (a) Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, or such longer period as is reasonably necessary if the failure cannot be cured within thirty (30) days and Tenant diligently pursues the cure; or (c) Tenant abandons the Premises.

9.2 Remedies. Upon an event of default, Landlord may terminate this Lease or re-enter the Premises and relet them for Tenant's account, in each case in addition to any other remedies available at law.

SECTION 10. MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Maine.

10.2 Quiet Enjoyment. So long as Tenant performs its obligations, Tenant shall peaceably enjoy the Premises without interference from Landlord.

10.3 Entire Agreement. This Lease, including its exhibits, contains the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant and may be amended only in a writing signed by both.
